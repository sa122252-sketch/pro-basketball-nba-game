### 🎯 **How Users Install on iPhone/iPad:**

#### **Step 1: Open in Safari**
- Open **Safari browser** on iPhone/iPad
- Go to your game URL

1. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow pointing up)

4. Tap **"Add"** in top-right corner

- Tap icon to launch

## ❓ **Troubleshooting:**

### "Add to Home Screen" not showing?
- ✅ Make sure you're using **Safari** (not Chrome)
- ✅ Must be on actual device (not simulator)
- ✅ iOS 11.3+ required

### Icon not showing?
- ✅ Clear Safari cache
- ✅ Remove and re-add to home screen
- ✅ Wait a few seconds after adding

### Not loading offline?
- ✅ Visit site once while online
- ✅ Service worker needs to cache files
- ✅ Check Safari settings allow data

### Touch controls not working?
- ✅ Make sure device isn't in zoom mode
- ✅ Close and reopen app
- ✅ Clear Safari cache and reinstall
